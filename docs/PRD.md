# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Vision
**Alamia Restaurant OS**: A multi-tenant SaaS enabling restaurants to receive direct orders over WhatsApp, manage kitchen operations, inventory, POS, customers, and marketing from one platform.

## Target Customers
- Small to medium food businesses (Pizza shops, Cloud kitchens, etc.)

## Monetization
- Setup fee: 2,000-3,000 PKR.
- Commission model: 1.5% commission on orders (High adoption rate vs Foodpanda's 20-30%).

## Core Principles
- Increase sales OR reduce staff workload OR increase repeat customers.
