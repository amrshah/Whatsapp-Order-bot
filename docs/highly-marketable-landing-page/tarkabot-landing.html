FAQ

Questions Restaurant Owners Ask

Can customers order in Roman Urdu?+

Yes. TarkaBot's AI understands Roman Urdu, standard Urdu, and English, and can even handle a natural mix of all three in the same conversation.

Does it work on my existing WhatsApp number?+

Yes. There's no need to change your number or ask customers to switch apps. TarkaBot connects to the WhatsApp Business number you already use.

Can I update my menu myself?+

Absolutely. The Menu Builder lets you add items, prices, and photos, and pause items that are out of stock, all without contacting support.

How long does setup take?+

Most restaurants are live within 30 minutes. Our onboarding team helps you load your menu and connect your WhatsApp number.

Can multiple staff use the dashboard at once?+

Yes. You can add staff accounts with role-based permissions, so kitchen staff only see the Kitchen Display while managers see full analytics.

Does TarkaBot support multiple branches?+

Yes. Each branch can have its own menu, WhatsApp number, and Kitchen Display, all visible from one owner dashboard.

What happens after my 14-day free trial?+

You can continue on the flat monthly fee with no setup cost, or cancel any time. No credit card is required to start the trial.
